# Mount art keys + rider anchor format (Art -> Engine / Render / Gameplay)

Rideable mounts are dedicated, true-scale art in the **nature** fragment (`packages/assets/pack/nature/manifest.nature.json`),
generated by `packages/assets/pack/nature/src/artpass_mounts.py` (renderer `mounts.py`). Seated rider anims live on every
v1 doll layer in the customization manifest (`ride_idle` / `ride_walk` / `ride_gallop`, generated by
`assets/customization/tools/ride_merge.py`). The anchor table is `packages/assets/pack/nature/mount_anchors.json`.

## Scale (single table, re-run to rescale)

All mounts are authored in metres and drawn at the player's pixels-per-metre:

* player doll `body_male` / `body_female` idle = **52 px** pivot -> head top (1x, all 8 facings) = **1.75 m**
* **PX_PER_M = 52 / 1.75 = 29.714 px/m** at 1x (@2x pages are exactly double)
* `mounts.py`: `PLAYER_HEIGHT_M`, `PLAYER_PX`, `SCALE_TABLE` (species -> sprite key, height m). Change them and re-run
  `python3 packages/assets/pack/nature/src/artpass_mounts.py --jobs 6` (needs `toktx` on PATH): pages, rects, seat
  anchors, saddles and `mount_anchors.json` all regenerate (the pass replaces its own previous output only).

lands near the doll's head line on the screen.

## Sprites

Per species `<sp>` in horse, donkey, moose, deer, elk, bison, yak:

| key | category | what |
|---|---|---|
| `mount_<sp>` | critter | the mount; anim frameMeta `df:<i>` -> `anchors.seat` |
| `mount_<sp>_fore` | critter | the mount parts nearer to the camera than the seat (head / neck / tail / near legs, depending on facing); drawn over the rider |
| `mount_saddle_<sp>_<tier>` | equipment | saddle overlay, `<tier>` in `leather`, `reinforced`, `iron`, `steel` (pixels hidden by the fore layer already removed) |

Draw order for a ridden mount at one pivot: `mount_<sp>` -> `mount_saddle_<sp>_<tier>` -> rider doll layers (in the
normal slot / layer order, anim `ride_<anim>`) -> `mount_<sp>_fore`. A riderless mount is just `mount_<sp>` (fore not needed:
it is a subset of the full sprite).

Saddle tiers: the art pass uses the four names requested (leather / reinforced / iron / steel). The gear tier ladder
elsewhere is bone / bronze / iron / steel / composite; map as gameplay sees fit (iron and steel share names).

## Anims, facings, timing

| anim | frames | fps | ms/frame | loop | rider anim |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| idle | 4 | 4 | 250 | yes | `ride_idle` |
| walk | 6 | 8 | 125 | yes | `ride_walk` |
| gallop | 6 | 12 | 83.3 (500 ms stride) | yes | `ride_gallop` |

8 facings, the same index order as the character dolls / creature sheets (engine dir 0..7 = E, SE, S, SW, W, NW, N, NE;
in the iso view: 0 front-right 3/4, 1 front, 2 front-left 3/4, 3 left side, 4 back-left 3/4, 5 back, 6 back-right 3/4, 7 right side).
Rider and mount frames are 1:1: play `ride_<anim>` frame `fi` with mount `<anim>` frame `fi` (same fps, same clock).
Bob sync: the mount seat anchor moves per frame (gait bob), the rider frame adds a small torso bob/lean on top.

Layout: rect dirFrames (`DirFrameJson.rect`) on shelf-packed pages, index `i = frame * 8 + facing`; each anim's
`dirFrames[i].anchor` is the mount pivot (centre between the hooves at ground level) relative to the trimmed rect.
`frames` = `[0..n-1]`, `dirStride` = n. Cells are trimmed with a 2 px clear guard. A layer that is fully hidden in a
facing (some fore/saddle cells) points at a shared 4×4 blank rect. Every non-blank cell is pixel-unique across all
species, layers and tiers (sha1 check in the generator). Binary alpha, flat cel fills + one hard shade + hard
highlight, bold soft-dark outline.

## Rider seat anchors

* Mount: `mount_<sp>.animations.<anim>.frameMeta["df:<i>"].anchors.seat = {x, y}` = px at 1x from the mount pivot
  (+x right, +y down), `i = frame * 8 + facing`.
* Rider: `body_male` / `body_female` `ride_<anim>` frameMeta `df:<i>` `anchors.seat` = the doll's seat socket in px from the
  doll's feet pivot (also has `handR` / `handL` on the reins). Male and female are identical.
* Place the doll pivot at `mountPivot + mountSeat - riderSeat` (= `riderOffset` in the table below).

`mount_anchors.json` (version 1):

```json
{
  "pxPerMetre": 29.7143, "playerHeightM": 1.75, "playerHeightPx": 52,
  "drawOrder": [...], "riderAnim": {"idle": "ride_idle", ...},
  "anims": {"idle": {"frames": 4, "fps": 4, "msPerFrame": 250, "loop": true}, ...},
  "tiers": ["leather", "reinforced", "iron", "steel"],
  "riderSeat": {"male": {"ride_idle": [[{"x", "y"} x 8 facings] x frames], ...}, "female": {...}},
  "species": {
    "horse": {"sprite": "mount_horse", "fore": "mount_horse_fore", "saddles": {"leather": "mount_saddle_horse_leather", ...},
               "wildSprite": null, "heightM": 1.6, "heightPx": 47.5, "heightNote": "withers",
               "seat": {"idle": [[{"x", "y"} x 8] x 4], "walk": [...6], "gallop": [...6]},
               "riderOffset": {same shape: seat - riderSeat, px; add to the mount pivot to get the doll pivot}}
  }
}
```

Arrays are `[frame][facing]`, 1x px; double for @2x.

## Rider doll (`ride_*`)

Every live v1 body / wear / hair / face layer (430 keys) has `ride_idle` (4 f, 4 fps), `ride_walk` (6 f, 8 fps) and
`ride_gallop` (6 f, 12 fps), loop, 8 facings, dirFrames on `cz_ride_<slot>_<n>` pages (customization shards).
Pages: 99 new `cz_ride_<slot>_<n>` pages (<=1024 @1x, PNG + tintmask + KTX2, @2x pages double), dirFrames index
`frame * 8 + facing`, pivot = doll feet (24, 74 in the 48x80 1x cell; anchor 0.5, 0.925 as every doll anim). Body sprites
(`body_male`, `body_female`) carry frameMeta `df:<i>` anchors `seat` (+ `handR` / `handL` on the reins); clothing / hair /
face / eyes / hat / accessory layers share the body's pivot, so the body's seat anchor places the whole doll.
Motion per frame (so cells differ): idle = breathing bob + small head rock + blink/expressions; walk = rider bounce with a
side rock and forward lean wave, heels swinging; gallop = crouched jockey pose, larger bob, lean wave, heel swing, yells.
Pose: seated astride with bent knees, hands forward on the reins, the far leg hidden behind the mount in side and 3/4
facings (layers stay in the slot / layer order and keep their tint masks, like the block anims).

## Saddle icons

`saddle_leather`, `saddle_reinforced`, `saddle_iron`, `saddle_steel`: category `item`, atlas `nature_icons`
(32×32, @2x 64×64), frames 124-127; CSS `[data-icon="saddle_<tier>"]` in `apps/client/src/gameplay-panels.css`.
The bike part `item_saddle` is unrelated and unchanged.

|---|---|---|---|
| `mount.mount` | 2 | ~780 ms | stirrup creak, swing, saddle settle, snort |
